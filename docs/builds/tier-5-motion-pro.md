## Cost

### $10,000–$18,000

<small>Assumes: existing high-end PC, dedicated room. Displays included</small>

- "Motion lite" now exists at $7,000 to $9,000

## Target user

- This is the main hobby
- Real-world drivers training
- Chasing the F1 Arcade feel at home
- Skip if: not yet consistent on Tier 3 or 4 (motion adds immersion, not pace)
- Skip if: someone lives below you (actuators thump)

## Parts

- Full: Simucube 3 Pro base $1,599 (25 Nm) + wheel ~$1,000 (est) + Simucube ActivePedal Pro brake from $1,849 + throttle ~$400 (est) + D-BOX G5 4-actuator kit $6,200 to $8,250 + Sim-Lab P1X Ultimate rig $877 + seat ~$400 + 3× 32" ~$900 + stand ~$300 = ~$13,500 to $15,600
- Motion lite: Moza R25 Ultra $899 + CS Pro wheel $329 + mBooster active pedal set $949 + Moza HMA150 4-actuator kit $2,999 + P1X Pro $770 + seat + 3× 27" + stand = ~$7,100
- Seat mover: Tier 4 build + Next Level Racing Motion Plus $2,799 = ~$6,500 to $7,500
- PC only: motion reads PC telemetry
- Not included: PC, pallet freight, software licenses

## F1 Arcade comparison

- F1 Arcade: Vesaro rigs, D-BOX G5 actuators, custom rFactor 2
- Newer venues: Simagic Alpha Evo base, P1000 pedals, GT Neo wheel (per Simagic)
- So the venue is Tier 5 motion with roughly Tier 3 controls
- The D-BOX kit alone costs more than a whole Tier 4 rig
- This tier: same motion family, stronger wheel, better pedals

## Footprint

- ~210×180 cm with triples, plus clearance to move (est)
- 150 to 200 kg without driver (est)
- One to two days to build, weeks to tune

## What it feels like

- Chassis heaves, pitches, rolls (3DOF). No yaw: slides are hinted, not felt
- Kerbs and gear shifts hit the whole body
- Active brake: ABS pulse, pad feel, different per car
- Not faster. More real

## Pros / cons

- \+: closest thing to the venue or a pro simulator
- \+: D-BOX and Simucube hold resale value
- −: cost
- −: noise and floor vibration
- −: tuning time
- −: little lap time gain
- −: service means shipping heavy parts

## Next upgrade

- Nothing hardware
- Coaching and seat time

## Used-market alternative

- Simucube 2 Sport / Pro (new $999 / $1,299), D-BOX Gen 3 to 5, Heusinkveld Ultimate+ pedals
- Check: actuator knocking, controller box and cables present, motion software license transfers, E-stop works
- Avoid: undocumented DIY motion, kits missing controller or brackets, sellers who won't demo under load
