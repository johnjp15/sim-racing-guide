## Cost

### $500–$700

<small>Assumes: existing PC/console, display, foldable space</small>

- PlayStation with direct drive: $900 to $1,100

## At a glance

| Spec | This tier |
|---|---|
| Mount | Folding seat, or a wheel stand with your own chair |
| Wheelbase | 4 to 6 Nm direct drive |
| Pedals | In the bundle, spring brake |
| Screen | The one you own. Works in front of a couch TV |
| Space in use | 176×93 cm (NLR GTLite Pro) |
| Folded | 104×93×30 cm, 14.4 kg. Closet, not under the bed |
| Setup | 1 to 2 min to unfold with gear attached (est) |

## Target user

| Good fit | Skip if |
|---|---|
| Hooked after Tier 0, or sure you'll stick with it | A 60×140 cm floor spot can be permanent. A fixed cockpit costs $100 to $200 more than a foldable and does not flex (Tier 2) |
| Apartment or shared room, the rig must disappear | You want a stiff load cell brake. Foldables flex |
| Console on a couch TV | |

## What it feels like

- Fixed seat-to-pedal distance, reclined posture, pedals stay put
- 5 Nm direct drive: smooth and detailed, still light
- Far less rigid than a fixed rig

## Pros / cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Packs into a closet | Flex under hard braking and above ~8 Nm (makers claim 10 to 13 Nm) |
| Real driving position | Fabric seat sags over time |
| Works in front of a couch TV | Shifter mounts wobble |
| Base, wheel, pedals all move to a rigid rig later | Cables to manage every session |
| Enough for years. r/simracing owners run 5 Nm on a folding stand and stay happy | Center post between the legs on some designs |
| | The foldable itself has no upgrade path |

## Next upgrade

- First: a load cell brake, set light. Foldables flex past ~25 to 30 kg
- Then: a rigid rig, once the space exists
- Resale on the foldable: about half (est)

## Used-market alternative

| | |
|---|---|
| Look for | Playseat Challenge, NLR GTLite, F-GT Lite. Common on Marketplace, ~$100 to $180 (est) |
| Check | Seams and velcro, hinges lock under load, wheel plate holes match your base, no bent tubes |
| Avoid | No-name stands made for a G29 only. Thin plates crack with direct drive |
