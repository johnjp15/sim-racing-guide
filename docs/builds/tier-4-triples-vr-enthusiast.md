## Cost

### $3,500–$5,500

<small>Assumes: existing PC with a strong GPU. Displays included</small>

- GPU for triple 1440p: RTX 5070 Ti class, ~$860. Pimax Dream Air class VR: RTX 4090 / 5090

## At a glance

| Spec | This tier |
|---|---|
| Mount | Heavy aluminum profile rig |
| Wheelbase | 12 to 21 Nm direct drive |
| Pedals | Mid load cell, plus a clutch |
| Screen | Triple monitors, or a VR headset |
| Extras | Shifter, two bass shakers |
| Space | 27" triples ~180×160 cm. 32" triples ~210 cm wide. VR ~150×70 cm (est) |
| Build time | A full day, including monitor alignment and software |

## Target user

| Good fit | Skip if |
|---|---|
| PC driver who wants peripheral vision and immersion | Mostly F1 25, Forza or EA WRC. They only stretch across triples |
| Rally and drift fans who need a shifter and handbrake | Console only: no triples. PlayStation has PS VR2 for Gran Turismo 7, otherwise a big TV on a Tier 3 rig is the ceiling |

## Triples vs VR

| | Triples | VR |
|---|---|---|
| View | 140 to 170°, cars visible alongside | True depth and scale |
| Comfort | Nothing on your face. Sit down and drive | Hot, isolating |
| Space | A room | None extra |
| PC load | High | Very high |
| Games | Not all. F1 25, EA WRC and Forza only stretch | Most sims |

- r/simracing in 2026: more people move from VR to triples than back. Comfort, not picture
- Try VR before buying. [Quest 3S][q3s] $350 is the cheap test
- Monitors, headsets, full comparison: [Displays](../components/displays-vr.md)

## What it feels like

- Look into the apex, see the car next to you
- Shakers: engine, kerbs and gear shifts through the seat. A buzz, not movement

## Pros / cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Biggest immersion jump per dollar | GPU cost is not in the total |
| VR is the cheapest way to 1:1 scale | Triples eat a room |
| Everything carries to Tier 5 | VR is hot and isolating |
| | More software to maintain (SimHub, triple configs) |
| | Shakers travel through floors to neighbors |

## Next upgrade

- First: belt tensioner. Then: motion (Tier 5)
- Optional: stronger base, active brake pedal. Feel, not lap time

## Used-market alternative

| | |
|---|---|
| Look for | Used monitors and triple stands: plentiful, low risk |
| Rule | Same model and revision matters more than price |
| Price | Used Quest 3: ~$350 to $450 is fair (est) |
| Check | Dead pixels side by side, VR lens scratches, headset factory reset |
| Avoid | Mixed monitor models, tight 1000R curves, sun-burned VR lenses |
