## How components fit together

TODO: simple diagram — Platform → Wheelbase → Rim / Pedals → Cockpit; Display + PC alongside. See also [How It Works — The 5 Pillars](../05-how-it-works.md).

- The **wheelbase** creates force feedback; the **rim** is what you hold and swap.
- **Pedals** — especially the brake — are the biggest consistency upgrade.
- The **cockpit / mounting** holds everything rigid; flex eats detail.
- **Displays / VR** decide immersion, space, and how powerful a PC you need.
- **Shifters, handbrakes, and accessories** are usually add-later items.
