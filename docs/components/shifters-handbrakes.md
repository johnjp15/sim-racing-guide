## Who needs one

| You drive | Shifter | Handbrake |
|---|---|---|
| F1, GT3, prototypes | No. Paddles | No |
| Modern rally | Sequential or paddles | Yes |
| Drift | H-pattern or sequential + clutch | Yes |
| Road cars, classics | H-pattern + clutch. The whole point | No |
| Trucks | H-pattern | No |

## Types

| Type | What it is |
|---|---|
| H-pattern | A gate with 6 to 8 positions, like a manual road car. Needs a clutch pedal |
| Sequential | Push for one direction, pull for the other |
| Dual-mode | Does both, by flipping a lever or swapping a plate |

## H-pattern levels

| Level | Price | Feels like |
|---|---|---|
| Toy | $40 to $60 | Light, plastic, no weight to the throw |
| Budget | $80 to $160 | Works, clear gates, still light |
| Mid, well loved | $130 to $200 | Proper click and resistance |
| Upper mid | $300 to $370 | All metal, heavy throw |
| Endgame, mechanical | $760 to $1,550 | Real gearbox feel |
| Endgame, active | $2,550 to $3,000 | A motor adds gear lockout and vibration |

## Sequential levels

| Level | Price | Feels like |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Paddles on the wheel. What most race cars use |
| Entry | $130 to $170 | Clicky, light |
| Mid | $200 to $270 | Solid thunk |
| Upper mid | $400+ | Heavy, magnetic snap |
| Endgame | $550+ | Mechanical, like a race car |

## Handbrake levels

| Level | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | A button on the wheel. On or off. Rally and drift want analog |
| Cheap USB | $30 to $100 | Fine for rally and drift. ~$30: on / off, a lottery. ~$60: decent. ~$90: load cell, good |
| Entry | $100 to $170 | Analog: partial lock |
| Mid | $110 to $230 | Load cell: pull by pressure |
| Endgame | $330+ | Load cell, all metal |

## Picks and warnings

| Product | Verdict |
|---|---|
| SHH HUBB | The enthusiast pick. One-person maker in Spain, good support. Replaced the Thorn in early 2026, all metal |
| SHH wait time | 3 to 6 weeks quoted, some waited months. ~$200 delivered to the US |
| Simagic DS-8X | Great H-pattern, weak sequential mode. Some owners report missed gears |
| Moza HGP | Strong centering, long throw. A plastic sleeve on the shaft wears and Moza sells no spare |
| Thrustmaster TH8A | Dated. Buy used or not at all |
| BDH H2SQ | Owners love the feel, question the price. A refurbished H1SQ is the value pick |
| BDH Active | Better, "not 3x or 4x better" than the mechanical one, per an owner |
| Moza active shifter (AB9 base + knob, ~$430) | Reviewed poorly so far |
| Logitech Driving Force Shifter | Works with Logitech G29 / G920 / G923 only |

## What matters

- Weight and click of the throw. That is all a shifter is
- A mount that does not flex. Shifters get shoved sideways
- H-pattern: a clutch pedal to go with it
- Handbrake: analog for drift and rally. A button only does full lock
- Premium handbrakes feel nicer. They do not work better
- Console: same brand as the wheelbase

## Ignore

- Gear count. 6 + reverse covers almost everything
- An expensive handbrake
- Buying one to start circuit racing
- An H-pattern without a clutch pedal

## Compatibility

| Platform | Rule |
|---|---|
| PC | Any USB shifter or handbrake with any wheel |
| Console | Must plug into the same brand's wheelbase |
| Logitech Driving Force Shifter | Logitech G29 / G920 / G923 only |
| Fanatec ClubSport SQ | The one premium shifter that works on console |
| Thrustmaster TH8A on PS5 | Only a short list of games |
| Moza, Simagic, Heusinkveld, SHH, BDH, Pro-Sim | PC only |

## By tier

| Tier | Shifter and handbrake |
|---|---|
| 0 to 3 | None, unless you drive road cars, rally or drift |
| 4 | Mid shifter + handbrake |
| 5 | Endgame |

## Used

- Check: every gear engages, no double shifts, lever play
- Thrustmaster TH8A: sensors last. Better value used than new
- Bring a laptop and test each gear
