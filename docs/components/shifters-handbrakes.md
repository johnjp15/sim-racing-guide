# Shifter & Handbrake

![example](../images/components/shifter.jpg)

- What: H-pattern shifter and handbrake lever
- Why: the cars that need them need them. rally, drift, old road cars
- When: you drive those cars regularly. paddles cover the rest

## Who needs one

| You drive | Shifter | Handbrake |
|---|---|---|
| F1, GT3, prototypes | No. Paddles | No |
| Modern rally | Sequential or paddles | Yes |
| Drift | H-pattern or sequential + clutch | Yes |
| Road cars, classics | H-pattern + clutch. The whole point | No |
| Trucks | H-pattern | No |

## Types

- H-pattern: a gate with 6 to 8 positions, like a manual road car. Needs a clutch pedal
- Sequential: push for one direction, pull for the other
- Dual-mode: does both by flipping a lever or swapping a plate

## What matters

- Weight and click of the throw. That is all a shifter is
- A mount that does not flex. Shifters get shoved sideways
- H-pattern: a clutch pedal to go with it
- Handbrake: analog for drift, button is fine for rally
- Console: same brand as the wheelbase

## Ignore

- Gear count. 6 + reverse covers almost everything
- Buying one to start circuit racing
- A shifter without a clutch pedal

## Compatibility

- PC: any USB shifter or handbrake with any wheel
- Console: must plug into the same brand's wheelbase
- Logitech Driving Force Shifter: Logitech G29 / G920 / G923 only
- Fanatec ClubSport SQ: the one premium shifter that works on console
- Thrustmaster TH8A on PS5: only a short list of games
- Moza, Simagic, Heusinkveld, SHH, BDH, Pro-Sim: PC only

## By tier

- Tier 0 to 3: none, unless you drive road cars, rally or drift
- Tier 4: mid shifter + handbrake
- Tier 5: endgame

## Used

- Check: every gear engages, no double shifts, lever play
- Thrustmaster TH8A: sensors last. Better value used than new
- Bring a laptop and test each gear
