## Buttons: levels

| Level | What | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | Wheel buttons + keyboard | $0 | Enough to start |
| Free | Old phone or tablet + SimHub | $0 | Touch dash and buttons over Wi-Fi |
| Desk | Elgato Stream Deck, 15 keys | $150 | Labeled LCD keys. Flat, so poor by feel and in VR |
| Entry box | Etsy, AliExpress, small makers | ~$80 to $200 | Real toggles and knobs. Works in VR |
| DIY | Arduino or Leo Bodnar board + switches + printed case | Parts only | Popular first project |
| Brand | Moza, Simagic, Fanatec, Sim-Lab boxes and dashes | $150 to $350 | Matches your wheelbase |
| Endgame | Apex Sim Racing and similar | $500+ | Screen, lights and buttons in one |

## Dash displays: levels

| Level | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Phone + SimHub | $0 |
| Entry | Small USB screen (VoCore based) from Etsy | $130 to $190 |
| Brand | Moza CM2, Simagic MagicDash, Sim-Lab DDU | ~$150 to $300 |
| Premium | Apex Sim Racing dash | $500+ |

## What matters

- Controls you use mid-race within reach: brake bias, traction control, pit limiter, radio
- Knobs and toggles you can find by feel, especially in VR
- Mount that does not wobble
- Wheel-mounted: reachable without letting go. Paid again on every wheel
- Rig-mounted: works with any wheel, cheaper. One hand leaves the wheel

## Ignore

- Button count
- Ignition key and starter button. Fun, not useful
- Buying a box and a dash on day one

## Sensible order

- Wheel buttons
- Phone dash (free)
- One rig-mounted box
- A dash screen, if wanted

## Other accessories

| Item | Worth it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fan aimed at you | Yes | Cheapest comfort upgrade. Needed in VR |
| Powered USB hub | Yes | Unpowered hubs cause dropouts mid-race |
| Rubber mat | Yes | Floor protection, noise |
| Cable ties, sleeves | Yes | Rig has 6+ cables |
| Gloves | With Alcantara wheels or strong bases | Sweat, blisters, wheel wear |
| Socks or thin shoes | Pick one, stay with it | Changes brake feel |
| Keyboard tray | On a rig | Menus and chat |
| Emergency stop | 15 Nm and up | Often included with strong bases |
| Wind simulator | Later | Fans tied to speed. Immersion |
| Head tracking | Trucks, flight | Rare in racing |

## By tier

- Tier 0 to 2: wheel buttons, phone dash
- Tier 3: one button box
- Tier 4: box + dash, tactile switches for VR
- Tier 5: full dash, wind
