# 2. Why Sim Race?

Purpose: Make the case honestly — benefits and trade-offs — so readers self-select before spending money.

## Key points to cover

- Lower cost / lower risk than karting and track days (no tires, brakes, travel, damage)
- Practice on demand: repeat a corner 50 times, race at night, any weather
- Skill transfer: braking, racecraft, car control fundamentals
- Structured competition: leagues, rating systems, esports pathways (overview only)
- Social side: leagues, clubs, racing with friends (the F1 Arcade feeling, at home)
- Cars/tracks you’d never otherwise drive

## The honest downsides

- Upfront cost and space (→ [Where & When](04-where-and-when.md))
- Time sink: practice, setup, tinkering
- It’s a rabbit hole: “one more upgrade” (→ [Buying Smart](09-buying-smart.md))
- No g-forces / real-world risk feedback — manage expectations vs. real driving
