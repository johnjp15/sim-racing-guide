# 3. Who Is This For?

Purpose: A reality-check chapter. Help readers decide if sim racing fits their habits before they buy — and which tier matches how they’ll actually play.

## Key points to cover

- Two reader types:
  - **Curious / casual:** loved F1 Arcade, wants to test the hobby cheaply
  - **Committed beginner:** already knows they’ll race weekly and wants to skip a throwaway purchase
- Questions to ask yourself:
  - Do you enjoy practice and gradual improvement, or only instant races?
  - Are you okay tweaking settings (FFB, FOV, bindings)?
  - How many hours per week, realistically?
  - Shared living space? Noise / vibration concerns?
  - PC, PlayStation, or Xbox already owned? (This constrains everything → [How It Works](05-how-it-works.md))
- Signs it’s probably not (yet) for you: no space, no patience for setup, expecting instant realism
- “Buy once vs. buy twice” framing: when a cheap starter is smart, when it wastes money (→ [Buying Smart](09-buying-smart.md))
