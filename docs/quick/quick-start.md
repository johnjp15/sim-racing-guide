## The 6 decisions, in order
1. **What do you want to drive?** — track, rally, drift, trucking, sandbox… see [Disciplines](../disciplines/index.md). This decides rim, pedals, shifter and seating position.
2. **Platform** — PC, PS5, or Xbox Series X|S? Console licensing is the #1 trap.
3. **Space** — desk only, fold away, or fixed corner/room?
4. **Seating position** — Formula (reclined) vs GT (upright-ish) vs rally/truck upright. Pick by primary discipline; GT is the best all-rounder.
5. **Budget** — gear only, assuming existing PC/console + TV unless a build says otherwise.
6. **Tier & build** — Tier 0–5 below, then the linked build page.

## 5-minute summary
| Question | Short answer |
|---|---|
| What did F1 Arcade give you home gear won't at first? | Full-motion seat, huge screen, zero setup, social head-to-head. Tier 0–1 will feel less spectacular; Tier 2+ beats it on force-feedback detail, braking consistency and seat time. Standard racing at F1 Arcade Chicago (1 W Grand Ave, opened Aug 2026, 61 full-motion sims) starts ~$19/session |
| Cheapest real start | $339–350 for wheel+pedals clamped to a desk (Tier 0) if you own PC/PS5/Xbox + TV/monitor |
| Upgrade that makes you faster | **Load-cell brake**, not more torque. From ~$149–160 (Moza SR-P2 / Logitech RS Pedals) |
| The trap | Console licensing. PS chip lives in wheelbase; Xbox chip in rim (Fanatec) or separate SKU. Moza/Simagic/Simucube/Asetek (except Moza R3-Xbox) are PC-only |
| Sweet spot | Tier 2 ($1,459, existing PC) or Tier 3 ($1,998, existing PC) |
| Need a gaming PC? | For iRacing, triples, VR, AC mods, LMU. PS5 = GT7 + ACC + F1 only, no triples/iRacing |
