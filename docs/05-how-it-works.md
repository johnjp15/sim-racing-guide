# 5. How It Works — The 5 Pillars

Purpose: Explain what a complete setup is made of, in the order a beginner should think about it. This is the “how” hub that every later chapter links back to.

## The 5 Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Platform & Games

- PC vs. PlayStation vs. Xbox: selection, compatibility, mod support
- The game/sim you pick drives hardware compatibility — TODO examples

### Pillar 2 — Controls (wheelbase + rim + pedals)

- Wheelbase = the motor that creates force feedback
- Rim = the steering wheel you hold (shape/size by car type)
- Pedals = throttle / brake / clutch; the brake is the performance part
- Detail: [Components](components/index.md)

### Pillar 3 — Cockpit / Mounting

- Desk clamp → wheel stand → foldable cockpit → fixed rig
- Why rigidity matters (flex eats force-feedback detail and load-cell braking)
- Detail: [Types of Setups](06-types-of-setups.md)

### Pillar 4 — Display

- Single screen, ultrawide, triples, VR — immersion vs. cost vs. space
- FOV (field of view) matters more than screen size — see [Glossary](glossary.md)

### Pillar 5 — PC, Audio & Accessories

- PC/console performance needs scale with display choice
- Headphones vs. speakers, button boxes, shifters, handbrake, keyboard tray, etc.
