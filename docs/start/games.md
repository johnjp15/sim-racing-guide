## Start here

| You are | Platform | Start with | Year-one cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 fan | PS5 / Xbox / PC | F1 25 + 2026 Season Pack | ~$75 to $150 |
| Casual | PS5 | Gran Turismo 7 | $30 to $70 |
| Casual | Xbox | Forza Horizon 6, Forza Motorsport on Game Pass | Game Pass or $70 |
| Casual | PC | Assetto Corsa + BeamNG.drive | $45 to $90 |
| Competitive, low budget | PC | RaceRoom (free) or ACC with Low Fuel Motorsport | $0 to $80 |
| Competitive, committed | PC | iRacing 3-month intro | $90 to $270 |
| Competitive | PS5 | GT7 Sport mode | $30 to $70 + PS Plus |

- Learn car control somewhere cheap before paying for iRacing. Skills transfer
- There is no F1 26. F1 25 plus the 2026 Season Pack is the current F1 game. Next full game: 2027
- F1 Arcade runs a custom rFactor 2. Closest at home: F1 25 for the cars, Le Mans Ultimate for the engine

## What iRacing really costs

- Year one, included cars and tracks only: ~$90
- Year one, with a normal amount of extra content: ~$160 to $270
- After: $125+ a year
- Try the 3-month intro (~$26) first
- Buy no extra content in month one. Rookie series need none

## Free ranked racing

- Low Fuel Motorsport: free license and rating system for ACC, Assetto Corsa, Automobilista 2, RaceRoom, Le Mans Ultimate
- Le Mans Ultimate: free tier of its own ranked system
- GT7 Sport mode: needs PS Plus

## Online basics

- Two ratings everywhere: one for speed, one for clean driving
- Clean laps raise the safety rating. Crashes lower it
- Ready for a first ranked race: 10 clean laps in a row, often 5 to 10 hours in
- Survive lap 1
- The overtaking car is responsible for a safe pass
- Spun: hold the brake, wait for traffic, rejoin off the racing line
- No retaliation. Report after the race
- Leagues: same people every week, cleanest racing for beginners
