## Desk or a dedicated spot?

- A spot where it stays set up gets used. Clamping and unclamping every session gets old fast
- A folding seat or wheel stand fixes most of it: sit down, pull the screen over, drive. [Tier 1](../builds/tier-1-foldable-apartment.md)
- A desk limits torque, and desks vary a lot
- At a desk, office chairs roll back under braking, and the seating position is hard to get right
- Fixes if you stay at the desk: [Desk fixes](../components/chassis-mounts.md#desk-fixes)

## Which game first?

- Assetto Corsa, PC, $20. Endless mods, any car, any track, free roam
- More options: [What to Play](games.md#start-here)

## Will my gear work together?

<!-- CHECK: Home and Tier 0 call a used Logitech G29 a cheap trial. Decide which view the site takes -->

- Skip gear and belt wheels. Start with a direct drive wheelbase
- PC is easiest. Few brands support console. Details: [PC / Console](../components/pc-console.md#console-wheel-rules)
- Brand matters some, but buy the part you want. One brand's wheels fit its own bases; other brands' wheels need an adapter: [Wheel](../components/wheel-rims.md#compatibility)
- On PC, pedals and shifters from any brand plug in by USB
- Brands: Moza runs from low to high end, so an all-Moza setup is a safe default. Simagic is mid to high. Simucube is high end only
- Install SimHub. Almost every setup ends up using it: [First Setup](first-setup.md#free-software-worth-installing-pc)

## How much?

- Buy in this order: wheelbase, wheel, pedals. [Buying Smart](buying.md#upgrade-order)
- About $500 for a decent start with a shifter:

| Setup | Torque | Total |
|---|---|---|
| Moza R5 bundle $379 + HGP shifter $149 | 5.5 Nm | ~$530 |
| Moza R9 V3 bundle $559 + HGP shifter $149 | 9 Nm | ~$710 |

- More than 5 Nm is worth it if the budget stretches. Do not overspend before you know you like it

<!-- CHECK: these bands differ from the Wheelbase page ("5 to 8 Nm is plenty to start, 12 the sweet spot"). Pick one set and the site will match it -->

| Torque | What you get |
|---|---|
| 3 to 5 Nm | Fine to start, little detail |
| 5 to 12 Nm | Decent, can clip in hard corners |
| 12 to 17 Nm | Enough for most people |
| 17 to 25 Nm | Everything comes through |
| 25 Nm+ | More than anyone needs |

- For scale: road cars are ~2 to 3 Nm at the wheel, GT3 race cars ~6 to 12. [Wheelbase](../components/wheelbases.md#what-matters)
- The mount limits torque: desk low, folding seat low to mid, rigid rig anything. [Chassis & Mounts](../components/chassis-mounts.md)
