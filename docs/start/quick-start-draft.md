## Desk or a dedicated spot?

- Get a spot where it stays set up. Clamping and unclamping every session is a pain, and you will drive less
- I had a Logitech G29 from my third year of college and barely used it, because attaching and detaching it was annoying. That changed with a folding seat: sit down, move the monitor, drive
- A desk limits torque. Some desks hold 12 Nm, others break at 5 Nm
- At a desk your chair rolls back when you brake (worst on wood floors), and your seating position is probably off unless chair and desk both adjust
- Fixes if you stay at the desk: [Desk fixes](../components/chassis-mounts.md#desk-fixes). Next step up: a folding seat or wheel stand, [Tier 1](../builds/tier-1-foldable-apartment.md)

## Which game first?

- Assetto Corsa, PC, $20. Think Garry's Mod for sim racing: mods, any car, any track, free roam
- More options: [What to Play](games.md#start-here)

## Will my gear work together?

<!-- CHECK: Home and Tier 0 still call a used Logitech G29 a cheap trial. Decide which view the site takes -->

- Skip Logitech. Not good enough to be good value. Do not buy anything that is not direct drive
- Go PC only if you can. Few brands support console. On console: Fanatec, Moza on Xbox through the R3 Xbox bundle, Moza PS5 bases announced. Details: [PC / Console](../components/pc-console.md#console-wheel-rules)
- Brand matters some, but buy the exact part you want. On one brand, new wheels just fit. Other brands' wheels need an adapter: [Wheel](../components/wheel-rims.md#compatibility)
- On PC, pedals and shifters from any brand plug in by USB
- Brands: Moza goes from low to high end, so an all-Moza setup is a safe default. Simagic is mid to high. Simucube is high end only
- Install SimHub. Almost every setup ends up using it: [First Setup](first-setup.md#free-software-worth-installing-pc)

## How much?

- Buy in this order: wheelbase, wheel, pedals. [Buying Smart](buying.md#upgrade-order)
- About $500 for something decent with a shifter:

| Setup | Torque | Total |
|---|---|---|
| Moza R5 bundle $379 + HGP shifter $149 | 5.5 Nm | ~$530 |
| Moza R9 V3 bundle $559 + HGP shifter $149 | 9 Nm | ~$710 |

- Go above 5 Nm if the budget stretches. A weak base holds you back once you are hooked. Do not overspend before you know you like it

<!-- CHECK: these bands differ from the Wheelbase page ("5 to 8 Nm is plenty to start, 12 the sweet spot"). Pick one set and the site will match it -->

| Torque | My take |
|---|---|
| 3 to 5 Nm | Fine to start, not much detail |
| 5 to 12 Nm | Decent, but it clips: a curb hit in a fast corner does not come through |
| 12 to 17 Nm | Good enough for most people |
| 17 to 25 Nm | You feel everything. You do not need more |
| 25 Nm+ | Do not even look |

- For scale: road cars are ~2 to 3 Nm at the wheel, GT3 race cars ~6 to 12. [Wheelbase](../components/wheelbases.md#what-matters)
- Where it mounts limits torque: desk low, folding seat low to mid (it twists), a rigid rig anything. Stiffer is better: the feel goes into your hands, not the frame
