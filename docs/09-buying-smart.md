# 9. Buying Smart

Purpose: Help readers spend in the right order, avoid compatibility traps, and decide new vs. used — the chapter that saves people money.

## Key points to cover

### Buying order

- Proposed priority to validate in research: rigid mounting + pedals before raw wheelbase torque
- What to buy first, what can wait (shifter, handbrake, button box, second rim)
- “Skip-a-tier” math: when buying once is cheaper than upgrading twice

### Compatibility traps

- Platform licensing: PC vs. PlayStation vs. Xbox wheels/bases are not all interchangeable
- Ecosystem lock-in: rims, quick releases, pedal connections per brand
- Cockpit mounting patterns and torque limits (strong base on a weak stand)
- Display/PC matching: triples and VR need a PC that can drive them
