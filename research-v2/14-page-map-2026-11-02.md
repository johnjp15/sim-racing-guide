# Page map: what each page is for, and where each topic lives

Not part of the built site. From the 2026-11-02 reorganization pass. Keep it current when a page changes job or a topic moves.

## Rule

- One home per topic. Other pages keep only their own specifics, plus one bullet linking to the home section (`page.md#anchor`)
- Tier pages share a template on purpose: that is not duplication
- Moving or renaming a heading breaks incoming `#anchor` links: grep `docs/` for the anchor and check with a strict build that sets `validation: links: anchors: warn`
