## Motion: degrees of freedom

| Axis | Movement | Feels like |
|---|---|---|
| Pitch | Tilts nose up / down | Braking, acceleration |
| Roll | Tilts left / right | Cornering load |
| Heave | Whole rig up / down | Kerbs, bumps, crests |
| Yaw | Rotates around vertical axis | Rear stepping out (traction loss) |
| Surge | Slides forward / back | Braking and acceleration, without tilting |
| Sway | Slides left / right | Cornering, without tilting |

- 2DOF: pitch + roll. Seat movers
- 3DOF: + heave. Four actuators under the rig. The usual home system
- 4DOF: 3DOF + rear traction loss (yaw)
- 6DOF: all six
- Fanatec's explainer: surge and sway add little alone. Yaw is the big step for feeling slides
- Most explainers come from sellers. Treat "more DOF is better" with care
- OC Racing: a static rig may suit serious competition better. Motion makes braking less repeatable
