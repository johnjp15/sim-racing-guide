# Research library (NOT published)

Purpose: raw research for the guide's author to write from in their own voice.

## For future sessions (including future Muse sessions)
- Read files in this folder and synthesize; do not copy wholesale into `docs/` without the author's direction.
- The author writes all wiki prose themselves. Research files are facts, tables, options, and sources — not finished copy.
- Prices are USD, checked on the date noted in each file (mostly 2026-10-02), and are volatile. Re-verify before publishing any price.
- Items marked Unverified / TODO were not confirmed against a live source. Do not publish them as fact.
- "Verified live" = checked on a manufacturer/retailer page at research time. "Index" = search/manufacturer listing only.
