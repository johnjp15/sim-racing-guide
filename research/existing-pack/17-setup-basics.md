## Setup Basics — suggested slug: setup-basics
**Page purpose (1 line)**
Ergonomics, FOV, FFB clipping, pedal calibration — short factual notes.

**Key facts:**
- Ergonomics: seat slightly reclined like road car; knee bend at full brake; wheel centre ~chest height, elbows ~90–120° at 9-and-3; screen 50–70 cm, centre at eye level (report)
- FOV formula in report: `horizontal FOV = 2 × arctan( (screen width ÷ 2) ÷ eye distance )`
- Clipping: peaks flatten above base capability; fix in report — turn game FFB down until peaks rarely clip, base at 80–100%
- Report comparison: 9–12 Nm at 70% vs 5 Nm at 100% on clipping/detail

**Tables ready to copy:**
- No table in report Part F. Copy-ready notes:
  - Brake deadzone ~2–5%
  - Calibrate max force so comfortable full press = 100%, start ~30–40 kg on load cell (report)
  - Throttle 0% deadzone at rest
  - Re-calibrate after elastomer/seat changes
