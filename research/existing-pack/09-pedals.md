## Pedals — suggested slug: pedals
**Page purpose (1 line)**
Sensor types and why load-cell brake is prioritised in report.

**Key facts:**
- Potentiometer/Hall brake = travel position; load cell = force/pressure (report)
- Report: load cell is single change most credited for braking consistency (Heusinkveld/iRacing guidance indexed)
- 2-pedal for modern GT/F1/rally paddle cars; add clutch only for H-pattern/trucks
- Load-cell requires rigid mount — will push chair/pedals on carpet
