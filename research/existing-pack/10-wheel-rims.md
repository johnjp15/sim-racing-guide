## Wheel Rims — suggested slug: wheel-rims
**Page purpose (1 line)**
Rim shapes, sizes, and quick-release compatibility.

**Key facts:**
- QR ecosystems not cross-compatible in report: Fanatec QR2, Moza QR, Simagic QR, Logitech Pro QR, Thrustmaster systems differ
- Rim only works on own base family without adapters (report)
- Button box budget $100–250 Est. in report, not verified; most beginners don't need one in year one

**Tables ready to copy:**

| Rim type | Diameter | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Round | 280–330 mm | Road, rally, drift, trucks | One rim to do everything; Logitech RS Round, Moza ES/CS |
| GT (cut-top/bottom) | 280–300 mm | GT3/GT4, general sim | Moza KS/CS, Fanatec McLaren GT3 class |
| Formula (open) | 270–280 mm | F1, prototypes, GT with many controls | More encoders/buttons; terrible for rally/trucking |

**Options/alternatives:**
- Round rim as all-rounder per report table
- GT rim for GT3/GT4, Formula rim for F1/prototypes
