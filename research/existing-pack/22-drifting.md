## Drifting — suggested slug: discipline-drifting
**Page purpose (1 line)**
Controlled oversteer — initiate, hold angle on throttle/handbrake, link corners.

**Key facts:**
- Scored on angle/line/speed in tandem; throttle balance and counter-steer speed are the skill, not lap time
- Round rim larger 330–350 mm (community sizing) for hand-over-hand and letting rim spin through palms
- Handbrake essential to initiate/extend slides; analogue strongly preferred (Sim-Lab, index)
- H-pattern + clutch near-essential for AC drift cars / CarX tuned cars (clutch-kick; general drift practice)
- Wheelbase must self-steer fast — wheel spins itself back in transitions; weak/slow bases or high damping are classic frustration; rotation 900°+
- Load-cell brake less critical than track racing
- Seating: GT/rally upright-ish, arm freedom for big wheel work; formula recline fights you
- Display: VR or ultrawide helps judge angle/look at exit; CarX often chase cam on single screen
- Budget fit: T1 + handbrake + clutch add-on is real entry; CarX console is cheapest console route, AC drift = PC required
