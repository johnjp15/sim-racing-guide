## Driving Practice — suggested slug: discipline-driving-practice
**Page purpose (1 line)**
Low-speed traffic-law driving — junctions, parking, lane discipline and observation habits, not racing.

**Key facts:**
- Hard caveat: no game here is driver-ed training or a licence substitute; familiarity only
- No source read evaluates real-world transfer — make no efficacy claim for practice sims in the wiki
- Gear for manual familiarity: 900°+ round rim, 3 pedals + H-pattern (clutch control is the skill); indicators/lights on buttons/stalks (enthusiast add-ons)
- Display: triples/VR/head-tracking earn their keep for mirror and shoulder-check habits; VR exists in original CCD (Steam page, index)
- Wheelbase torque minimal — cheap entry wheel beats expensive DD for this use
- Seating: upright road-car (GT/truck-like), pedals low like a road car; reclined formula tub teaches wrong posture
- Budget fit: T0–T1 suffices; do not sell a T2 rig "for learning to drive"
