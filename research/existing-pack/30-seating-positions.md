## Seating Positions — suggested slug: seating-positions
**Page purpose (1 line)**
Formula, GT, Rally and Truck seating geometry — and why Formula-to-GT switching on one rig is bolt work, not a lever flip.

**Key facts:**
- Four things must move for a proper Formula ↔ GT change: (1) seat base angle/height, (2) pedal tray height + angle, (3) wheel deck height + tilt, (4) monitor height/FOV (eyes move ~a head-height)
- NLR F-GT manual F1→GT steps (primary document, index): remove pedal plate from pedal-angle bar and remount on front plate; install GT support posts both sides to raise seat; adjust seat angle, wheel angle and wheel position — unbolting/re-bolting with tools, not quick release
- Aluminium-profile rigs: T-slot is adjustable with a hex key on every axis — workshop task, not quick-release; seat sliders solve fore-aft/driver height only, not position class
- Industry sells the in-between as a named position: NLR describes hybrid as "seat in GT position but pedals in a formula style position" (NLR F-GT page, index)
- Practical advice from report: buy GT as all-rounder; add formula flavour with a formula rim, not a formula seat, unless F1/open-wheel is >~80% of driving (editorial threshold); pick seat by primary discipline

**Tables ready to copy:**

| Position | Seat | Legs / pedals | Wheel | Matches | Long-session comfort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Formula | Very reclined, seat base low | Legs raised, feet near seat height; pedals high and angled toward driver | Low, close, angled up toward chest | F1/open-wheel in iRacing, AMS2, F1 games | Worst for most adults; in/out is a manoeuvre |
| GT | Semi-upright bucket, moderate recline | Feet lower than hips, pedals below seat level, near-vertical pedal faces | Chest height, arms slightly bent | GT3, touring, Hypercar, most track, drift, weaving, oval — the default | Best all-round; endurance stints designed around it |
| Rally | Upright, seated higher than GT | Pedals lower, more knee bend for bracing | Higher and closer (leverage) | Rally, rallycross, off-road | Good; easy in/out |
| Truck | Upright and high, road-car/truck chair height | Pedals low and flatter, like a van | Larger rim, more tilted toward driver | ETS2/ATS, driving practice, cruising | Best for multi-hour sessions with reclining seat |

**Options/alternatives:**
- Pedal risers / angled pedal brackets can fake formula pedal height on a GT rig (generic accessory class, no specific product verified)
- Second seat (formula shell + GT recliner) possible on profile rigs; doubles cost and storage
- Two drivers sharing: seat slider + marked pedal/wheel positions beat "switchable position" marketing
