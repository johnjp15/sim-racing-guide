## Discipline Traps — suggested slug: discipline-traps
**Page purpose (1 line)**
Gear and game mistakes that only appear after you pick a discipline — check before buying.

**Key facts:**
- Most traps are platform or rim mistakes: formula rim for rally/truck/drift, PC-only base for PS5, console plans built on PC-only games
- Verified Oct 2026 platform core: CCD + CCD 2.0 PC only; No Hesi PC only; BeamNG PC + PS5 from 19 Oct 2026 (no mods/VR at launch, no Xbox); ETS2/ATS PC only to buy today, console announced no date
- Load-cell brakes push you backwards — fixed rig/seat matters more than Nm; high-Nm base on desk/foldable flexes
- Motion/haptics add immersion, not speed (review consensus, index) — buy last, for fun
