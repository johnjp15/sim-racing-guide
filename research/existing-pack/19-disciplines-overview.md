## Disciplines Overview — suggested slug: disciplines-overview
**Page purpose (1 line)**
Choose gear by what you want to drive first — discipline changes rim, pedals, shifter, handbrake, display and seating.

**Key facts:**
- Discipline sections below each cover: what it is, main games/platforms Oct 2026, gear that matters, seating, display/VR, budget fit
- Corrections vs old assumptions preserved from research: AC Rally in Steam Early Access since 13 Nov 2025 (PC); BeamNG PS5 Early Access announced for 19 Oct 2026 (no mods/VR at launch, price unannounced); ETS2/ATS console announced but no date in Oct 2026; LMU console early–mid 2027; iRacing PC only
- Report discipline tier shorthand (T0 controller/desk, T1 entry DD, T2 mid, T3 high) is a discipline-suitability label — build anchor totals stay in the Tier 0–5 build sections, do not mix the two numbering systems without noting it
- One rig can cover multiple disciplines (see One Rig, Multiple Disciplines), but seating position is the hard part to swap (see Seating Positions)

**Options/alternatives:**
- Start with your primary discipline; add the second discipline's add-ons later (handbrake, H-pattern, second rim)
- Do-everything compromise from report: 300 mm round/D-shape rim, GT seating, 3-pedal load-cell set, mid DD with 1080°+ rotation available
