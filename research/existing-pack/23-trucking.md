## Trucking / Hauling — suggested slug: discipline-trucking
**Page purpose (1 line)**
Long-haul delivery and light business sim — smooth low-speed manoeuvring, trailer reversing, long relaxed sessions.

**Key facts:**
- No racing: skill is smooth manoeuvring, reversing a trailer, route/fuel management; earn, buy trucks/garages, hire AI drivers
- High rotation, low torque: set wheel ~900° for ETS2/ATS (Steam Community marked answer, index); entry base fine, big-Nm DD wasted here
- Large round rim for slow hand-over-hand parking; formula rims miserable
- Buttons matter: lights, wipers, indicators, retarder, engine brake, cruise, cameras — button box / many-button wheel beats keyboard
- Shifter: automatic/paddles fully playable; H-pattern with range-splitter + clutch is enthusiast immersion, not required
- Handbrake: no — parking brake is a button
- Seating: truck position upright and high, wheel tilted toward you; reclining/road-car-style seat beats bucket for 2–4 h sessions
- Display: ultrawide/triples for mirrors, VR/head-tracking for shoulder checks; modest frame-rate demands
- Budget fit: T0–T1 honestly enough; spend on comfort (seat, button box, screen), not torque
