## Rally — suggested slug: discipline-rally
**Page purpose (1 line)**
Point-to-point timed stages vs the clock — car placement and pace notes, not wheel-to-wheel racecraft.

**Key facts:**
- One mistake ends the run; surfaces change gravel/tarmac/snow; rallycross is the circuit exception in DR2.0/EA WRC
- Analogue handbrake is closest to essential: initiates rotation and tightens line; button loses modulation (Sim-Lab, index)
- Round rim 320–330 mm for hand-over-hand slide catching; formula rim has no top to grab (community sizing)
- Modern Rally1/2 = sequential stick/paddle; historics = H-pattern + clutch; 3 pedals for historics, 2 fine for modern
- Wheelbase: fast free self-steer and slew matter more than peak torque; mid 5–9 Nm, 900–1080° rotation (general practice)
- Seating: rally position — upright, higher than GT, wheel closer/higher for leverage
- Display: VR strong where supported for crests/distance; single/ultrawide fine; triples are wasted width here
- Budget fit: T1 works (DR2.0 cheap/complete); discipline spend is handbrake add-on (~$99 class, see One Rig topic); T2 for AC Rally historics with H+clutch
