## Discipline-to-Gear Matrix — suggested slug: discipline-gear-matrix
**Page purpose (1 line)**
One-table summary: what each discipline actually needs, and what it does not.

**Key facts:**
- Key in report: essential/core, nice/situational, not needed — condensed below to Essential / Nice / No
- Handbrake initiates rotation (rally/drift only — Sim-Lab, index); load-cell = pressure-based braking repeatability (track — ISRTV, index)
- Round rims exist because rally/drift/truck require hand-over-hand; ETS2/ATS want 900° and gentle forces; triples = 170–210° FOV for side traffic, VR = depth at 90–130° (index)
- Rim diameters and rotation norms are community/expert consensus (OverTake forums), not manufacturer standards — word as "typical" in wiki
