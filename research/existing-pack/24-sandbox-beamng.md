## Sandbox / Physics Play — BeamNG.drive — suggested slug: discipline-sandbox-beamng
**Page purpose (1 line)**
Soft-body physics sandbox — crash tests, freeroam, scenarios, off-road, drift and mods; experimenting is the game.

**Key facts:**
- Every panel/suspension part simulates and deforms in real time; freeroam, scenarios, deliveries, chases, time trials, Career mode
- PC: Steam Early Access since 29 May 2015, still Early Access, no 1.0 date (index)
- PS5: launches in Early Access 19 Oct 2026 (announced 24 Sep 2026), 4-player split-screen, FSR3/PSSR Pro; no mods, no VR at launch; price not announced as of 1 Oct 2026 verification — at 2 Oct read date upcoming, not yet playable
- Xbox: nothing announced
- Gear: almost none specifically — fully playable on controller/keyboard; wheel adds fun, FFB tuning famously fiddly (community)
- Round rim + handbrake nice for drift/off-road play; load-cell, button box, big torque irrelevant
- Seating/display: whatever is comfortable, single screen standard; VR on PC via OpenXR (single-source guide)
- Budget fit: T0 controller is a legitimate full experience — cheapest discipline in pack to enjoy properly
