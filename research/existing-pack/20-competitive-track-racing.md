## Competitive Track Racing — suggested slug: discipline-competitive-track
**Page purpose (1 line)**
Closed-circuit wheel-to-wheel racing — the closest step-up from F1 Arcade.

**Key facts:**
- Loop: qualifying + race, lap time and racecraft; licences/ratings in iRacing/ACC/LMU online
- Sub-flavours: GT3/GT4, Hypercar/LMP endurance, open-wheel/formula, touring/stock
- Load-cell brake is the #1 upgrade: load cells measure pressure, position pedals measure travel (ISRTV, index)
- Modern GT3/formula/Hypercar use paddles — H-pattern/clutch unused in those classes
- Rim buttons/rotaries matter mid-corner: brake bias, diff, TC/ABS, engine mode
- Wheelbase 5–8 Nm usable, 8–12 Nm headroom to avoid clipping kerb/ABS detail; rotation low ~540–720° (typical sim setting, general practice)
- Seating: GT position for GT3/touring/Hypercar; most home drivers stay GT even for formula cars (community norm)
- Display: triples = 170–210° physical FOV, best side-by-side awareness; VR = 90–130° with depth; single 16:9 still most-used in OverTake 2026 poll (index)
- Budget fit: starts T0/T1 discipline shorthand; serious iRacing/ACC = T1 minimum sensible, T2 sweet spot; T3 for triples/VR endurance
