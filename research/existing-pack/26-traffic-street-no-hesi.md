## Traffic Weaving / Street — suggested slug: discipline-traffic-street
**Page purpose (1 line)**
High-speed lane-splitting through dense highway traffic on PC servers; console equivalent is Forza Horizon open world.

**Key facts:**
- "Cutting up" through traffic, scored socially/by server points; night-highway Tokyo Shuto aesthetic (Shutoko Revival Project)
- No Hesi / SRP / traffic mods require modded Assetto Corsa + Content Manager/CSP on PC — No Hesi does not exist on console
- Gear: round rim 300–330 mm, 900° rotation; paddles or H+clutch car-dependent — 3-pedal + H is flexible setup (general)
- Handbrake nice for tight gaps/style, not essential; fast self-steer matters like drifting
- VR popular to judge gaps (head movement), triples also work; single screen is hard mode (community pattern)
- Seating GT upright; budget floor is T1 PC build — discipline does not exist below PC/mod level
- Safety note for wiki: game loop only, never present as real-road behaviour to copy (editorial)
