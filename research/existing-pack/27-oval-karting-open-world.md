## Oval, Karting & Open-World (Brief) — suggested slug: discipline-oval-karting-open-world
**Page purpose (1 line)**
Three smaller disciplines that mostly run happily on the generic GT setup.

**Key facts:**
- Oval/NASCAR: sustained high-speed banked-oval pack racing, momentum/drafting/spotter awareness; iRacing (PC) is sim home
- NASCAR 25 (iRacing Studios): PS5/XS 14 Oct 2025, PC 11 Nov 2025, US $59.99 standard (iRacing.com + Speedway Digest, index)
- Oval gear: round/larger rim, steady wheel work; triples/VR for pack beside you; 2 pedals + paddles/sequential; GT upright; T1+ (console NASCAR 25 on entry wheel fine)
- Karting sims: KartKraft (Windows PC only), Kart Racing Pro (PC), KartSim for rFactor 2 (PC), karts in AMS2 (PC) and GT7 (PS) (index)
- Kart gear: direct steering, tiny rotation, no gears except shifter-karts — small rim, 2 pedals, strong immediate FFB at low Nm (general kart characteristics); low reclined kart posture is niche, do not build for this alone
- Open-world cruising: FH5/FH6, BeamNG freeroam, AC freeroam mods (PC), ETS2/ATS as cruising — whatever you own works, round rim + comfortable upright setup beats race parts, controller fine
