## Concise Layer / Equipment Finder — suggested slug: equipment-finder
**Page purpose (1 line)**
Skim layer: pick a tier/build in ~5 minutes without reading detailed pages.

**Key facts:**
- F1 Arcade Chicago: 1 W Grand Ave, 61 full-motion sims, standard racing from ~$19/session — home Tier 0–1 less spectacular, Tier 2+ beats it on FFB detail, braking, seat time
- Cheapest real start: $339–350 complete wheel+pedals, desk clamp, existing PC/PS5/Xbox + display
- Biggest speed upgrade: load-cell brake pedal (~$149–160 pedal sets), not more torque
- Trap: console licensing — PS chip in wheelbase, Xbox chip in rim / separate Xbox SKU; Moza/Simagic/Simucube/Asetek PC-only except Moza R3 Xbox
- Sweet spot in report: Tier 2 ~$1,459 / Tier 3 ~$1,998, existing PC
- PC required for: iRacing, triples, VR, AC mods, Le Mans Ultimate
