## Cockpits & Mounting — suggested slug: cockpits-mounting
**Page purpose (1 line)**
Mount types, torque limits, rigidity and footprints.

**Key facts:**
- Report rigidity rule: pedal rigidity matters more than wheel rigidity for load-cell consistency
- Seat progression in report: fabric foldable < recliner < fixed bucket for load-cell braking
- Motion must be decided before rig in report: retrofitting expensive, platforms need compatible rigs

**Tables ready to copy:**

| Mount | Safe torque | Flex reality | Price anchor | Footprint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Desk clamp | ≤5 Nm comfortable | Desk dependent; monitor shake | Included | Desk |
| Wheel stand (NLR 2.0) | ~8–10 Nm (Lite 2.0 rated 10 Nm) | Good wheel deck, chair still moves | **$279** / Lite $149 | ~100×60 cm |

**Options/alternatives:**
- Desk, wheel stand, foldable, steel tube, aluminium profile — see table
- Motion-ready heavy profile if Tier 5 plausible (Tier 4/5 topics)
