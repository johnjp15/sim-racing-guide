## 0. Cross-discipline facts (read first)

- **Platform gates everything.** iRacing, Automobilista 2, AC Rally, No Hesi (AC PC mods), BeamNG mod scene and City Car Driving are PC-only. PlayStation's serious entry is GT7 (+PS VR2); Xbox's is Forza Motorsport/Horizon + NASCAR 25. Console buyers must buy a *licensed* base — see the platform lock notes in "One rig, multiple disciplines" below.
- **Load-cell brake is the only near-universal upgrade.** It matters most in competitive track racing; it helps everywhere except pure cruising/trucking (index: ISRTV load-cell explainer).
- **Analogue handbrake is essential only in rally and drift** (it initiates rotation with modulation a button can't give) (index: Sim-Lab handbrake collection page).
- **Rotation tells the story:** formula/GT cars ~540–720°, rally/drift ~900–1080°, trucks/karts ~900°+ (karting much less). A base that can do 1080°+ and be software-limited down covers all disciplines (community consensus, OverTake).
- **Rim diameter/size by discipline is community consensus, not a manufacturer standard** (OverTake forums, index): formula 280–300 mm, GT ~300, rally 320–330 round, drift 330–350 round, truck 300–330+ large round, kart 260–280 small.
- **Display:** triples (170–210° physical FOV) earn their cost in pack racing (oval, track, No Hesi traffic gaps); VR (90–130°, depth judgement) earns it in rally and traffic weaving; trucking/cruising are fine on a single screen or ultrawide. Single 16:9 was still the most-used display in an OverTake 2026 poll (index).
- **Seating default is GT** (semi-upright). Formula position is a bolt-work conversion on most rigs; NLR F-GT proves it with its own manual steps (primary document, index). Full geometry table in §14.

### Gear

- **Essential:** load-cell brake pedal; paddle shifters (modern GT3/formula/Hypercar); rim with buttons/rotaries (brake bias, TC/ABS, diff, engine map); 5–12 Nm DD (8–12 gives clipping headroom).
- **Optional:** button box (endurance: lights, wipers, pit limiter, radio); second formula rim; triple screens or VR; bass shakers.
- **Not needed:** H-pattern, clutch (except historic classes), handbrake.
- **Seating:** GT position (formula position optional flavour, rarely worth it at home).
- **Display/VR fit:** triples = side-by-side awareness; VR = depth; both "essential-tier" for serious endurance. VR supported in iRacing, ACC (PC), AMS2, LMU (SteamVR listing), GT7 (PS VR2) — *check per-title patch parity before buying a headset for one game* (flagged in prior research).

## 2. Rally

**What it is:** point-to-point stages against the clock; pace notes, surface changes (gravel/tarmac/snow); one mistake ends the run. Rallycross (in DR2.0/EA WRC) is the circuit exception.

### Games and platforms

| Game | PC | PS | Xbox | Status Oct 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Assetto Corsa Rally | Steam Early Access | No | No | EA since 13 Nov 2025; v0.3 added winter/snow tyres (BoxThisLap, index); $29.99 (Steam via BoxThisLap/BSIMRACING, index). v0.6 Sep 2026 per prior research (index). 1.0 timing UNVERIFIED |
| DiRT Rally 2.0 | Yes | PS4 | XB1 | 2019, still the cheap complete package; VR PC only, no PSVR (OverTake, index) |
| EA Sports WRC | Yes | PS5 | XS X\|S | Official WRC licence; VR = PC SteamVR beta only, no PSVR2 (index, prior research) |

### Gear

- **Essential:** analogue handbrake (initiates rotation); round rim 320–330 mm (hand-over-hand slide catching — a formula rim has no top to grab); sequential shifter for modern Rally1/2 (stick or paddles).
- **Optional:** H-pattern + clutch for historics (→ 3 pedals); bass shakers; VR.
- **Not needed:** triples (wasted width), big torque (fast self-steer/slew rate beats peak Nm; 5–9 Nm mid DD typical).
- **Seating:** rally position — upright, higher, wheel closer/higher for leverage.
- **Display/VR:** VR strong where supported (crests/distance); single/ultrawide fine.

### Cost to start

- Games: DR2.0 complete and cheap (exact Oct 2026 price TODO); AC Rally $29.99; EA WRC one-time (price unverified this pass).
- Discipline add-on: analogue handbrake — MOZA HBP $99 (MOZA US store, verified live 2026-10-02 in prior research).

### Community

- DR2.0 clubs + EA WRC official esports; AC Rally leaderboards growing in EA. Smaller, more solo/time-trial culture than track racing — community size numbers UNVERIFIED this pass.

## 3. Drifting

**What it is:** judged slides, not lap time — car control at big slip angles, tandem runs online.

### Games and platforms

| Game | Platform | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Assetto Corsa (original, PC mods) | PC only for mod scene | The drift standard — huge mod car/track library (prior research, index) |
| CarX Drift Racing Online | PC + consoles | Accessible, arcade-leaning; CarX 2 status UNVERIFIED |
| BeamNG.drive | PC; PS5 EA from 19 Oct 2026 | Physics sandbox drifting; PS5 launch has no mods/VR (RacingGames.gg, index, verified 1 Oct 2026 by that source) |

### Gear

- **Essential:** round rim 330–350 mm; analogue handbrake; clutch pedal (clutch-kick); 900°+ rotation; base with fast, free self-steer (slew > peak Nm).
- **Optional:** H-pattern (most drift cars) or sequential; VR (looking out the side window mid-slide).
- **Seating:** GT or rally upright both fine.
- **Cost to start:** AC is a ~$20-class one-time buy, frequently far less on sale (Steam search showed AC at $4.99 on a -75% sale in an Oct 2026 index snapshot — *sale price, volatile, do not publish as the price*); handbrake add-on $99 class (MOZA HBP, verified live 2026-10-02).

### Community

- Discord-based tandem servers and mod communities (PC). Console drift scene is mostly CarX lobbies. No verified size numbers — UNVERIFIED.

## 4. Trucking (ETS2 / ATS)

**What it is:** long-haul delivery + light business sim. Skill = smooth manoeuvring, trailer reversing, route/fuel management. Sessions are long and relaxed; there is no racing.

### Games and platforms (Oct 2026 — updated this pass)

| Game | PC | PS5 | XS X\|S | Oct 2026 status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Euro Truck Simulator 2 | Yes (Steam) | Not yet | Not yet | PS5 version publicly playable at Tokyo Game Show Sep 2026; **still no release date** (Univers-Simu, index). Console editions will bundle DLC at a discount, editions/price unannounced (TGS 2026 producer interview via YouTube summary, index — treat as secondary) |
| American Truck Simulator | Yes (Steam) | Not yet | Not yet | Same console situation. ATS hit a new concurrent-player record (21,318 Steam) after the Ford Car Pack DLC + Road Trip mode (Traxion, Oct 2026, index) |

**Wiki-safe statement Oct 2026:** ETS2/ATS are PC-only to buy today; console versions are real and playable behind closed doors/at shows, with no date — do not publish any 2026 key-shop "31 Dec 2026" dates (placeholders, AllKeyShop index).

### Gear

- **Essential:** high rotation (set ~900°, Steam Community marked answer, index), large round rim 300–330+, *many buttons* (lights, wipers, indicators, retarder, engine brake, cruise, cameras — button box or many-button rim beats keyboard).
- **Optional:** H-pattern with range-splitter + clutch (immersion, not required — automatic/paddles fully playable); head-tracking for shoulder checks; ultrawide/triples for mirrors.
- **Not needed:** handbrake (parking brake is a button), high torque (low Nm fine; a big DD is wasted money here).
- **Seating:** truck position — upright/high, reclining road-car-style seat beats a bucket for 2–4 h sessions.
- **Cost to start:** games are one-time Steam purchases; ETS2's decade of map DLC is the real cost driver (bundled editions on Steam; console bundles planned, price UNANNOUNCED). Exact game prices TODO — verify on Steam.

### Community

- TruckersMP (unofficial multiplayer mod, PC) + SCS Convoy mode; huge, non-competitive community. Size numbers UNVERIFIED this pass.

## 7. Traffic / street weaving (No Hesi)

**What it is:** cutting through dense highway traffic at speed on modded Assetto Corsa servers (Shutoko Revival Project, Tokyo expressway).

### Platforms/status

- PC only (AC + Content Manager + SRP mods). SRP Discord showed **445,893 members** in an OverTake page snapshot (index, Oct 2026) — report as "SRP Discord in the mid-400-thousands (Oct 2026 index snapshot)", not a live count.
- No Hesi runs its own servers/Discord on top of SRP (prior research). Member counts UNVERIFIED this pass.

### Gear

- **Essential:** round rim 300–330; VR *or* triples (gap judgement in traffic is the whole game); wheel that self-steers fast.
- **Optional:** handbrake (play), H-pattern or paddles depending on car.
- **Seating:** GT. **Cost to start:** AC ($20-class, sale-dependent) + free mods; SRP content via Discord (Patreon optional support, Patreon page index).
- **Console note:** there is no No Hesi equivalent on console; Forza Horizon traffic is the closest vibe (arcade physics).

### Swap-ability (what actually changes between disciplines)

| Swap | Easy? | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Rim via quick release | Yes — **within one brand's QR system** | QR systems are proprietary: Fanatec QR2 ≠ QR1 ≠ MOZA ≠ Simagic. A rim does not click across brands without adapters (and adapters usually lose native buttons/FFB pass-through) |
| Clutch pedal add-on | Yes, modular pedal sets | Cheaper bought as a 3-pedal set up front |
| USB handbrake / shifter / button box (PC) | Yes | Brand-agnostic USB HID on PC (Thrustmaster TSS is a discrete USB device — Tom's Hardware, index) |
| Pedals/shifters across brands (PC) | Yes | Separate USB devices |
| Anything mix-and-match on console | **No** | Add-ons must run through the licensed base; base must be PS/Xbox-licensed. MOZA's first PlayStation base was only *announced* at Tokyo Game Show Sep 2026, unpriced (Traxion, index) |
| Seating formula ↔ GT | **No** | Bolt work: seat angle, pedal tray, wheel deck, monitor height all move (NLR F-GT manual, index) |
| Rotation / torque profile per game | Yes | Software profiles — the one-base answer is 8–12 Nm, 1080°+ available, limited per game |

## 13. Discipline-first buying order (research summary)

1. Pick **platform** first (PC vs PS vs Xbox) — it gates the base, which gates everything.
2. Pick **primary discipline** → rim shape, pedals, handbrake/shifter needs (matrix §12).
3. Load-cell brake before torque; handbrake before a second rim (rally/drift); buttons before torque (trucking).
4. Display spend follows discipline: triples only if pack racing (oval/track/No Hesi); VR if rally/traffic; neither for trucking/cruising.
5. Seating: GT unless one discipline is >~80% of your driving (editorial threshold from prior research).
