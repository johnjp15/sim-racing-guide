    Tier 0: Desk Starter

    Cost

    $200–$400

    Assumes: existing PC/console, display, desk

    At a glance

    Spec This tier
    Mount Desk clamp, your own chair
    Wheelbase 4 to 6 Nm direct drive. PlayStation: 2 to 3 Nm gear drive
    Pedals In the bundle, spring brake
    Screen The one you own
    Space Your desk. Packs away into one box
    Setup 10 to 15 min the first time, a few minutes each session (est)

    Target user

    Good fit Skip if
    Curious, want to try a wheel at home for minimum money Desk is wobbly or glass
    Shared room, no dedicated space Already sure you want a rig (go to Tier 2)
    Not sure it will stick

    Parts

    Platform Pick Price Notes
    PC Moza R5 bundle $379 5.5 Nm direct drive, wheel, 2 pedals, desk clamp
    PC, newer Moza R5 Pro bundle $399 6 Nm, newer base and pedals. Take it if the gap is small
    PC, cheaper Moza R3 bundle $279 3.9 Nm direct drive
    Xbox Moza R3 Xbox bundle $339 Also works on PC
    PlayStation Logitech G29 ~$190 to $230 Gear drive, 3 pedals. $300 list, never pay list
    Xbox, cheapest Logitech G920 ~$200 to $230 Same wheel, Xbox version
    PlayStation, smoother Thrustmaster T248 $300 to $330 Belt / gear hybrid, 3 pedals
    • No PlayStation direct drive at this price
    • Shifter is not in the box. Not needed to start

    What it feels like

    • Direct drive (Moza): smooth, quiet, light
    • Gear drive (Logitech): notchy, loud gear rattle
    • No fixed seat, so the chair and pedals can move
    • Slower than a controller for the first week or two
    • Unlimited seat time

    Pros / cons

    Pros Cons
    Cheapest way in Office chair rolls back under braking
    Nothing to build Pedals slide on hard floor
    Resells easily Brake is a spring, not a load cell
    Moza parts bolt onto any rig later Clamp on, clamp off every session. Friction kills the habit

    Free fixes

    Problem Fix
    Chair rolls Caster cups, or rear wheels in old shoes
    Pedals slide Against the wall, or screwed to a board the chair sits on
    Desk shakes Push the desk to the wall, lower force feedback

    Next upgrade

    • First: a load cell brake. Moza SRP2 pedals $149. Stopgap: Moza brake kit $29
    • On a desk: brake set light, chair blocked, pedals against the wall
    • Then: stop things moving. Wheel stand or foldable (Tier 1), or a rig (Tier 2)
    • Carries over: Moza base, wheel, pedals
    • Does not carry over: Logitech and T248 sets are closed systems, sold whole. Their next step is a direct drive bundle

    Used-market alternative
    Look for Logitech G29 / G920, ~$100 to $150 local
    Why Resells for what you paid: a free trial
    Check Centers on power-up, no grinding, all pedals reach full range, power brick included
    Avoid G27 and older (no PS5 / Xbox), anything without force feedback
