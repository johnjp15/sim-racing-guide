    Tier 5: Motion / Pro

    Cost

    $10,000–$18,000

    Assumes: existing high-end PC, dedicated room. Displays included

    • "Motion lite" now exists at $7,000 to $9,000

    At a glance

    Spec This tier
    Mount Heavy aluminum profile rig on four motion actuators
    Wheelbase 25 Nm direct drive
    Pedals Active brake, good throttle
    Screen Triple 32", or a high-end VR headset
    Extras Belt tensioner
    Space ~210×180 cm with triples, plus clearance to move (est)
    Build time One to two days to build, weeks to tune

    Target user

    Good fit Skip if
    This is the main hobby Not yet consistent on Tier 3 or 4. Motion adds immersion, not pace
    Real-world drivers training Someone lives below you. Actuators thump
    You want the rig to move under you

    Parts

    Build What Total
    Full Simucube 2 Pro $1,299 + wheel $649 + active brake $1,849 + throttle $499 + Link Hub $150 + rig with D-BOX motion $6,384 + bucket seat $399 + belt tensioner $1,839 + triple stand $359 + 3× 32" monitor $750 ~$14,180
    Motion lite Moza R25 Ultra $899 + CS Pro wheel $329 + mBooster active pedal set $949 + Moza HMA150 motion $2,999 + TR160 V5 rig $730 + seat $399 + stand $359 + monitors $750 ~$7,410
    2DOF, whole rig moves Tier 4 build on an NLR Elite rig + NLR Motion Plus $2,799 ~$6,500 to $7,500
    • PC only: motion reads PC telemetry
    • Belt tensioner: owners with both rate it above motion alone. Buy it first
    • Newer base: Simucube 3 Pro $1,599. Its wheels are harder to find in stock
    • Not included: PC, freight, software licenses

    What it feels like

    • The rig heaves, pitches and rolls (3DOF). No yaw: slides are hinted, not felt
    • Kerbs and gear shifts hit the whole body
    • Active brake: ABS pulse, pad feel, different per car
    • Not faster. More real

    Pros / cons

    Pros Cons
    Closest thing to a pro simulator Cost
    D-BOX and Simucube hold resale value Noise and floor vibration
    Tuning time
    Little lap time gain
    Service means shipping heavy parts

    Next upgrade

    • Nothing hardware
    • Coaching and seat time

    Used-market alternative
    Look for Simucube 2 Sport / Pro, D-BOX Gen 3 to 5, Heusinkveld Ultimate+ pedals
    Check Actuator knocking, controller box and cables present, motion software license transfers, E-stop works
    Avoid Undocumented DIY motion, kits missing controller or brackets, sellers who won't demo under load
